Open Terrace · Rooftop Experience

Ananta Street's open terrace is intended to be a vibrant yet laid-back rooftop area where people can eat, rest, and spend time together. The area produces a welcoming evening environment with its terrace restaurants, cafés, and outdoor seating. Beyond dining, it serves as a communal space where individuals connect and exchange experiences while taking in the views of the city.

Club & Lifestyle

The Sociètè Club · Lifestyle Destination

The Club at Ananta Street is intended to be a comprehensive lifestyle location that combines socializing, recreation, and fitness. The club finds the ideal balance between leisure and wellness by providing members with a revitalizing area to relax, mingle, and remain active.
